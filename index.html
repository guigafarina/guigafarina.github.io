About me

I'm Guilherme, but everyone calls me Gui. I've worked in Mexico, the UAE and Brazil, and I'm Brazilian with EU citizenship.

Most recently I worked on project finance for a US$500M ethane import terminal, a joint venture between Braskem and Advario in Mexico, covering insurance, debt calculations and lender reporting. Before that I worked in trade operations at a polymer and chemicals trader in Dubai, issuing shipping documents and writing the company's operating procedures.

I studied International Business at the University of Victoria, with an exchange semester at Singapore Management University. I'm now based in Calgary and building a career in commodity trading and shipping.

Outside work: soccer, hiking, travelling and reading.
